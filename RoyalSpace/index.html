A practice built around possibility.

Game designer, developer, story writer, and creative builder working between the page and the playable world.

I am interested in the moment an idea becomes a place someone else can enter. My work moves between narrative design, game systems, worldbuilding, software, and visual experimentation.

Some projects are games in active development. Others are lore archives, client work, short fiction, music, visual studies, or community-centered preservation work. Together they form one practice: making specific worlds and finding the right way for people to experience them.

Game development · Senior capstone

Ghibli Game

A four-person Boise State GIMM capstone directed by Jojo Noyes, with Boon Burns leading programming, Trey Bledsoe leading music and reactive audio, and Lily Pinson leading art and design. Inspired by Studio Ghibli, especially Spirited Away, the game follows a traveler arriving at a magical hotel and exploring its city, ponds, and bathhouse-like world through chores, puzzles, quests, memories, and character stories.

The team is committed to handcrafted art, audio, visuals, and story, with only limited AI-assisted code and no generative AI for player-facing content. The technical vision includes Unity URP toon shaders, low-poly painterly assets, Wwise integration, reactive motifs, streamed hotel zones, time-of-day schedules, cinematic cameras, branching dialogue, NPC behaviors, data-driven quests, inventory, accessibility, saves, and world-flag achievements.

  • Direction & story
  • Unity · Wwise
  • Handcrafted content
